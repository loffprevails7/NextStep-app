Support

You’re not alone in this.

If you need someone to talk to right now, or if the weight feels too heavy, these external resources are available. NextStep itself is a practical organization tool — not a counseling or crisis service.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    Call or text 988 (United States)
    988lifeline.org
  • Crisis Text Line
    Text HOME to 741741
    crisistextline.org
  • Local grief support
    A trusted funeral home, place of worship, or community organization can often point you to local bereavement groups.
